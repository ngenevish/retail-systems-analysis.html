**Retail Systems Analysis**

The adoption of a mixed-use property featuring both retail and residential units can be an attractive investment. A nearby attraction serving as a catalyst only makes the prospect more attractive--turning a loss leader into investment winner. There is a reciprocal relationship between the two entities—residents serving as a core customer base and attractive retail availability driving demand and rents. 

A retail-only build is certainly feasible—but relies too heavily on the sale of goods inside the store. In times of economic uncertainty, consumer confidence can decline and discretionary spending will drop. 

Uncertainties around office space—particularly in a post-pandemic environment—also exist. In fact, even minor changes to office demand can have detrimental impacts on a developer’s net operating income as well as the site’s land value. Given this, it is advisable that office space + retail is not explored without some level of public support and capital to ensure financial viability.
